# MyMusicTaste User Guide

## Navigation
At any time you can navigate between important pages through the navigation bar at the top of every page. From here you can get to the **Home Page**, the **Search Page** and the **Login/Signup** page.

#### Logged in users

For logged in users the **Login/Signup** button is replaced by the **Logout** button, which logs out the current user immediately.

## Search Page

**Users** and **songs** can be searched for using this page. Write your desired item in the textbox at the top of the page to see the most relevant results for each category. Switch to a *Songs* tab or a *Users* tab to see more results within those categories.

If you can't find the song you are looking for, you can add it to our database with the **Add it to our database!** link. This will redirect you to the **Song submission** page. You have to be logged in to see this page.

## Song page

On this page you can find the most important metadata about the given song:
- Title
- Author
- Album
- Genre
- Release date

Link to listen to this song can be found here as well, if provided.

Underneath are statistics about the song calculated from the ratings given by MyMusicTaste users:
- Total Listeners (users who gave this song a rating)
- Average rating
- Median rating
- Rating distribution (divided into buckets of 5)

At the bottom of the page is a comment section.

#### Logged in users
If you are logged in, you can add the song to your personal collection by clicking on the **Add to collection** button under the song's cover image. Once your song is in your collection, you can see the rating you gave it, change it by moving the slider, or remove it from your collection by click the **Remove from collection** button.

## User page

On this page you can find the user's **About me** section as well as their **collection** ordered by rating for each song. Clicking on any of the **Albums**, **Authors**, **Genres** tabs above the collection will show the user's favorite items in the category based on the average rating of its individual songs.

Underneath the collection is a comment section.

#### Editing user page

If you are logged in as the owner of the user page you can edit your profile by clicking on the **Edit button** in the upper right corner. You will enter an *edit mode*, where you can change your
 
- *About me* section - by writing into the textvox
- Profile picture - by clicking on it and entering a valid internet image address

The *edit button* will be replace by *Save* and *Stop editing* buttons. To save your changes click on *Save* button. To discard your changes click on *Stop editing* button without saving. This exits the *Edit mode*.

You can also **change rating** of songs in your collection by either clicking on the the rating value and overwriting it, or by drag-and-dropping above any other song in the list.

## Comments
Under each User and Song page is a comment section with the most recent comments. You can navigate to the user page of the comment poster, by clicking on their name or profile picture.

#### Logged in users
Logged in users can post a comment to any comment section by clicking on the **Post a comment**. This opens a new comment with editable content. Click on *Save* to save your comment. Click on *Close* without saving to discard the new comment.

Once the comment has been saved, you can safely close it, keep editing or delete it permanently by clicking the *Delete* button.
You can only post one comment at a time. If you want to post more, please refresh the page.

You can always edit your older comments by clicking on the *Edit* button in the upper left corner of your comment.

## Song Submission
You can get here from the **Search Page** if you're logged in. Enter the correct metadata for the song you want to add:
- Title - mandatory
- Author - mandatory
- Album
- Genre
- Release Date
- Cover Image Link - must be a valid internet image address
- Source Link - must be one of the accepted music providers

Title and Author must be a unique pair, not yet included in our database. Click on **Submit** to save this song, and you will be redirected to its new page.

The accepted music providers are YouTube, YT Music, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music
